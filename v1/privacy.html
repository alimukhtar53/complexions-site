Who We Are

Complexions Beauty Salon, Unit 1, Batsford Estate Yard, Batsford, Moreton-in-Marsh, GL56 9QF, is responsible for the personal information you share with us. We respect your privacy and only collect what we need to look after you.

What We Collect

When you book, call, email or visit us, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, email address and postal address
  • Your appointment and treatment history
  • Health and skin information from your consultation form, so we can carry out treatments safely
  • Information about how you use this website, collected through cookies

How We Use It

We use your information to manage your appointments, provide safe and suitable treatments, answer your enquiries and send appointment reminders. We’ll only send you news and offers if you’ve told us you’d like them, and you can opt out at any time.

We process your information to provide the treatments you book, for our legitimate interests in running the salon, and with your consent. Health information is only used with your explicit consent.

Who We Share It With

We never sell your information. We only share it with trusted providers who help us run the salon, such as our online booking system [Booking provider] and website host [Hosting provider], or where the law requires it.
